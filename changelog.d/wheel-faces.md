### Added

- **The 3D view's wheels show their own faces.** Where a wheel part's featured photo is one wheel seen face-on on white, the 3D view draws each wheel's outer face from it, trimmed to the wheel, and its sides in the tread's colour from the same photo. An angled shot, a set, a box or a coloured ground gives no face, and the wheel is drawn in its colour as before (D-572).
