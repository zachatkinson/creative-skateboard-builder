# Milestone 11 review: The motion

Tag `v1.1.0` · planned 20 November 2026 · written by Claude Code; reviewed by spec-reviewer, auditor, then Claude in Cowork.

## Plan

### What it builds

The roadmap's milestone 11: the Motion section of `docs/SPEC-CONFIGURATOR.md`, plus Cowork's review of 1.0.0 (#80 to #90, folded in with no 1.0.x release) and the issues carried in this milestone's GitHub milestone (D-042).

1. **Owed first: the two should-fixes from Cowork's 1.0.0 review.**
   - **#81, F2:** after a new width, the picked deck stays at the old width's variation, and a shopper can order the wrong size. How the build follows the width is Question 1.
   - **#80, F1:** a mouse press held while a region hydrates is lost. `release()` clicks the held control when the press ends over it, it is still in the page and no click reached it (`assets/src/builder/view.ts:102-114`).
2. **Cowork's minor findings.**
   - **#82, F3:** with no deck layer image, the deck's photo takes the board's place at its size. On tablets and phones, the stage isn't sticky until there is a board. The visual baselines change on purpose.
   - **#83, F4:** a search or chip that empties the list says so, not the fit rule.
   - **#84, F5:** the step's own noun on the chips and in Show more's status, through `_n()`, in French too.
   - **#85, F6:** a ready build with a part no longer offered gets one line per slot, in the tile's words.
   - **#86, F7:** "Back to the shop" and "Sign in to save this build" get 44 px on a phone.
   - **#87, F8:** the currency's separators are decoded as `format` is (D-290).
   - **#88, F9:** a build link keeps a `nofollow` that the site or another source set, in core's robots and in Yoast's.
   - **#89, F10:** the state answer stops carrying the step's cards three times. The view references the frozen raw fields; the schema only changes by addition.
3. **Carried issues.**
   - **#90:** a `Server-Timing` header on the state route (Question 3), so D-294's priming can be measured on a real host. Then the priming itself, if the header shows it pays.
   - **#73:** hydrate less at the start, measured before and after at 12 times slower.
   - **#75:** LCP headroom on CI's fixture, without loosening the 2,500 ms gate.
   - **#72:** `Words.php`'s two mbstring calls (Question 2).
   - **The flaky tests:** #36, #49, #70, #91 and #93, each fixed or kept quarantined with its reason. #49 (a pick's paint at 4×) and #93 (Firefox's hydration stall) bear on this milestone's speed gate and get looked at first.
4. **The Motion setting:** `motion` in `_cbuild_layout` (`schema/kit-layout.json`), playful or subtle. It goes in the Build tab's Layout section, the kit's export, the abilities and `docs/SETTINGS.md` (D-255).
5. **One motion module in the store.** It decides whether a moment moves, fades or changes at once, from `prefers-reduced-motion` and the setting. A new pick cuts any running moment short (`finish()` on its animations, `skipTransition()` on a view transition). Focus is kept across a transition. It uses no library, and the code counts toward the 15 kB budget.
6. **The ten moments** in the spec's table:
   - deck picked, trucks, wheels, swapped, removed, grip and Show the top move the board's parts with the Web Animations API, on `transform` and `opacity` only;
   - swap and Next step are view transitions, with a class for the direction where Firefox has no transition types;
   - Price changes is the counting total (D-257): its in-between frames are formatted from the route's currency and `aria-hidden`, and the last frame is the server's `wc_price()` string, said once by the status region;
   - Add to cart moves toward the Mini-Cart's button where the page has one, and otherwise fades the board in place.
7. **The tests the roadmap names:**
   - each moment's end state as a visual baseline;
   - each moment's duration, and its fade under reduced motion and subtle, read from `Animation.effect.getComputedTiming()` in the browser;
   - the speed gates: a pick within 100 ms at 4×, INP under 200 ms (from Event Timing entries, with no new tool), no layout shift, and a Chromium performance trace of each moment kept as a CI artifact;
   - accessibility: the total said once, the frames hidden, nothing moving under reduced motion, focus never lost;
   - engines: Firefox without transition types, and a browser without view transitions changing at once.
8. **The release:** French compiled, every changed baseline remade on purpose, upgrades from every release, the docs, `CHANGELOG.md`'s 1.1.0, and the audit.

### Questions for Zach

Answered on 22 November 2026: Zach took the recommendation on all three.

1. **#81: what happens to the picked deck when the width changes?** My recommendation is that the pick moves to the same look at the new width. The status and the card say so ("Hard Hat is now 8.5″"), and the change panel lists any later part that no longer fits. Where the look doesn't come in the new width, the ticked card stays and names its width, so the shopper can see what Next would keep. The other choice is that the new width's variation shows unticked and the shopper presses it again.
2. **#72: should mbstring be required, or should the code fall back without it?** My recommendation is to guard `Words.php` as `SpecTableEditor` and `WpTextFolding` already are, so no new requirement is added. The other choice is to require mbstring in the readme and the floors check and drop the guards, which also kills the last escaped mutant.
3. **#90: who should get the `Server-Timing` header?** My recommendation is a signed-in user who can manage WooCommerce, with no filter. Cowork could then measure on the demo from wp-admin, with no code on the host and no new public API. The other choices are a `cbuild_server_timing` filter, which is public API and needs code on the host to turn on, or `WP_DEBUG` only.
