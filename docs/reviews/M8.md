## Plan

Everything under Orders, Admin's On orders and Privacy, after what milestone 7 left owed and the two changes Zach asked for while reviewing the picture.

   - **#8, a deleted network site's tables.** Saved builds make these tables personal data from this milestone. A must-use file on a network names them for `wpmu_drop_tables` (Zach, 17 November).
   - **Cowork's recheck of 0.7.0** on the demo (`~/tmp/claude-review/M7-recheck.md`). Zach, 17 November: its fixes go into this milestone, not a 0.7.1.
     - **N1, should-fix:** "Read every part again" made no resized copies of layer images, though Site Health said it did. The reindex queues each such part's job, the update to 0.8.0 does the same with no step for the shop, and Site Health's wording matches what is so in each case (D-214).
     - **M6, minor:** with Yoast, `og:url`'s brackets were printed bare, since Yoast urldecode()s its filter's value. The filter hands Yoast the link in the form that decodes to the encoded one; the build link's format is unchanged (D-215).
     - **The upgrade notice** asked sites older than 0.6.0 to deactivate first. No site runs one, so 0.8.0's notice asks for no manual step (D-216).

2. **#46, the board flips** (Zach, 16 November; the design confirmed on 17 November). A top view drawn from the deck layer's own outline, grip filled in as a swatch, bolt heads at the holes, and the wheels at the edges. A "Show the top" button, and the grip step turns the board over. The composed picture stays the underside.
3. **Orders, for the customer:**
   - the order's lines grouped by build on the order page, My account and the emails, the parts under their kit;
   - the composed picture in the HTML emails, with the build's description as its alt text, and the description alone in plain-text emails;
   - the lead time and the returns notice in the emails, read from the kit item's snapshot (D-133);
   - emails in the customer's language, English and French.
4. **Orders, for the workshop:**
   - the **Build box** on the order screen, for `wc_get_page_screen_id( 'shop-order' )`, HPOS on or off. It lists the build by slot, with each part's specs as sold from `_cbuild_snapshot`, the layered picture and a Print assembly ticket button;
   - its **mismatch warning** when the order's lines no longer match the snapshot: a part removed, a quantity changed, a line copied without its keys, or a kit item with no `_cbuild_id` (D-139);
   - the **assembly ticket**, a print page with its own print styles and no PDF. It shows the order number, the customer, each slot's part and SKU with the specs that matter, any notes, and a tick box per step;
   - the **bulk print** from the orders list, one build to a page;
   - the orders list's **Assembly column**, on the HPOS list and the older one;
   - the **refund help text**: restocking unused parts when the grip is already applied takes two refunds.
5. **Saved builds under My builds:**
   - Save build on the review, for a signed-in customer. A signed-out shopper gets a link to sign in that brings them back to the same build;
   - My builds in My account: each build's picture, name, total today and the date saved, with Open, Rename and Delete;
   - each change carries a nonce and works without JavaScript;
   - owner-only, checked on every request;
   - opened as a way into the builder, rebuilt and checked again, so a part that's gone is named, as a build link does;
   - saving from a build that was opened from My builds updates it.
6. **Privacy:**
   - the personal data exporter and eraser for saved builds;
   - deleting an account deletes its saved builds, on a single site and on a network;
   - the suggested policy text, in English and French, through `wp_add_privacy_policy_content()`.
7. **Uninstall** keeps order item meta and removes saved builds (the table, already). Tested again with builds in it.

Not in this milestone: adding a build to an order by hand (not in 1.0), French read by a person (milestone 9), and the hallway test (milestone 9).

### The public API it adds

Each item goes into `docs/HOOKS.md` with `@since 0.8.0`, the snapshot and the changelog in the commit that adds it. Names are settled as they're built; these are the expected ones.

- **REST routes,** all for a signed-in customer and their own builds only, with the `wp_rest` nonce:
  - `cbuild/v1/builds`: `GET` lists the customer's builds; `POST` saves one;
  - `cbuild/v1/builds/(?P<uuid>…)`: `PATCH` renames it, `DELETE` deletes it.
- **The state route** gains fields and loses none: each slot's `recommended` part (#45), the total's `estimate` (#45), and the picture's top view (#46). `total` changes meaning: it counts only what was picked. That is a behaviour change to a public answer, recorded as a decision and in the changelog.
- **My account endpoint** `my-builds`, with its menu item.
- **The builder URL's `cbuild_saved` parameter,** the UUID of the saved build it was opened from. Honoured only for its owner, and never in a build link, Copy link or `og:url`.
- **Admin:** the bulk action `cbuild_print_tickets`, the print page's action, and the orders list column `cbuild_assembly`.
- **Privacy:** the exporter and eraser, both with the id `creative-skateboard-builder`.
- **Filters,** only where a shop needs them: the assembly ticket's specs per slot (`cbuild_ticket_specs`) and the ticket's steps (`cbuild_ticket_steps`), so a workshop can add a step without a template override.
- **Templates:** the ticket, the Build box, My builds and the email's build block are the plugin's templates, as the builder's are.

### Decisions I expect to record

- **#45 in the spec.** Recommendations are labelled, never picked; the total counts what was picked; the estimate is the total with each remaining slot's recommendation. The review and the cart refuse a build missing a required slot, as they do now for a slot left empty. A preset still fills every slot (P3), since the shopper chose it.
- **The picture in emails goes under the kit's line,** not in WooCommerce's thumbnail, which is 32 or 48 px and shown only with `email_improvements`. The JPEG at email width, linked by its URL in uploads, with the build's description as its alt. If the picture isn't made yet when the email is sent, the email shows the deck's photo, as the cart does. Plain-text emails get the description.
- **The mismatch is worked out when the Build box is shown,** by comparing the order's lines with the snapshot, so a change by any route is caught. No hook on saving order items.
- **The ticket is an admin page that prints,** behind `edit_shop_orders` and a nonce, one build to a page with `break-after: page`. No PDF, as the spec says.
- **Opening a saved build** is the builder's own URL with the picks and `cbuild_saved=<uuid>`. The UUID is honoured only for its owner, and dropped from Copy link and the share card, so a share link still carries part IDs only.
- **Saved builds per customer are capped,** at 100 to start, so the table can't be filled by one account. The number is recorded with its reason.
- **#46's design,** as in the issue, and the must-use file for #8.

### Questions for Zach

Answered on 17 November 2026:

1. **#8, the tables of a deleted network site.** WordPress drops a deleted site's tables only when a plugin loaded in that request names them (`wp-includes/ms-site.php:832`), and with the plugin active on that one site, none of its code is loaded when the network admin deletes it. **Zach chose a must-use file:** on a network, the plugin writes a small file to `wp-content/mu-plugins` that only names its tables for `wpmu_drop_tables`, and uninstall removes it. Where the folder can't be written, Site Health says so.
2. **#46, the flip.** **Zach confirmed the design as proposed in the issue,** in this milestone, after the orders work.
