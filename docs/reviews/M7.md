# Milestone 7 review: The picture

Tag `v0.7.0` · planned 14 November 2026 · written by Claude Code; reviewed by spec-reviewer, auditor, then Claude in Cowork.

## Plan

### What it builds

The board drawn as it is built, from one layout, in the page and as one composed image. Then what milestone 6 left owed.

1. **Owed first.**
   - **Cowork's should-fix from the 0.6.0 release check (`M6-release-check.md`, item 2).** Under Shop Kit's product template on a desktop, the builder's column is 592 px wide and centred in a 1,290 px row. The template gives that column `max-width: 560px` and auto margins. D-184's rule clears the width but not the margins. The fix is `margin-inline: 0` on the same selector, which Cowork tried on the demo: 1,242 px, with the summary beside the steps.
   - **Safari's first deck pick (D-185),** pressed by hand by Zach on the demo. It passes in Chromium on the demo and in WebKit in CI. A failure becomes a fix here.
   - **Issue #39:** the seeded Independent, Thunder and Venture charts gain each size's baseplate, from the makers' own pages, with the source and the day read. An upgrade fills only empty cells.
   - **Carried open issues:**
     - #40, the classic checkout's flaky Place order;
     - #36, Firefox's axe crash, quarantined;
     - #22, axe target-size on a variation's Part specs in webkit-phone;
     - #25, the mutants that survive outside the domain.
     Each is fixed or closed with its reason. All four move into this milestone.
   - **Milestone 6's minors that the picture touches:**
     - the assembly switch, which is a link and becomes a button in the step's form (auditor 29, spec M2);
     - "What is in the box", which is an `aria-label` and becomes a visible heading (auditor 27);
     - a check of focus and the picture's description after every step change, not just one (spec M6).
2. **The renderer interface,** with two renderers that draw the domain's `Layout` (D-060). One interface lets a 3D view be added after 1.0.
3. **In the page:**
   - The picture becomes layered `<picture>` elements, each with an explicit size and AVIF and WebP sources.
   - The deck layer is `fetchpriority="high"`.
   - Each layer carries `cbuild_part_classes`.
   - A pick swaps one layer in place with a view transition, and the description is said once.
   - A part without a layer image keeps milestone 6's labelled photo within the same picture.
   - The phone bar's small picture becomes the board.
4. **The layers' renditions.** A layer image is a transparent PNG or WebP from the Media Library. The plugin makes its AVIF and WebP renditions at the sizes the picture shows, without changing how WordPress handles the shop's other uploads.
5. **Composed:**
   - GD by default, and Imagick when the host has it.
   - JPEG for email and WebP everywhere else.
   - Made by `cbuild_compose_picture` in Action Scheduler, only for a build that passes validation.
   - Kept in `uploads/creative-skateboard-builder/pictures/`, named by a hash of the layers, the layout's version and the size.
   - Pruned daily by `cbuild_prune_pictures`: files unused for 30 days go, and the folder stays under its size cap.
   - The queue is capped.
   - A host with neither image library makes no composed picture. It shows the deck's photo and says so in Site Health.
6. **The admin:**
   - the Layer image field on a part's Part specs panel (simple products and variations), with a preview on the template at the part's size and checks for transparency and size;
   - the kit's Picture section on the Build tab (`_cbuild_picture`: the layer order and any anchor);
   - the Catalogue check's list of parts without a layer image.
7. **Where the composed picture goes:**
   - **the kit's cart thumbnail,** in the Store API (the Cart block, Checkout and the Mini-Cart) and the classic cart. The deck's photo shows until the picture is made.
   - **share cards:** `og:image`, `og:url` as the build link, and the build's name and total. They go through Yoast's filters when Yoast is active, and the plugin prints its own tags when no SEO plugin is active.
8. **`docs/PICTURE.md`,** the shot template for the agency's photographer: camera height and lens, scale, nose up, background, and the export settings for a transparent master.
9. **Uninstall** removes the pictures folder and the renditions.

Not in this milestone: the picture in emails, the Build box and the assembly ticket (milestone 8), and 3D (after 1.0).

Each item goes into `docs/HOOKS.md` with `@since 0.7.0`, the snapshot and the changelog in the commit that adds it.

  - `cbuild_compose_picture`, which composes one build's picture;

  - `_cbuild_picture` on a kit: the layer order and anchors;

### Decisions I expect to record

- **One interface, two renderers, no filter yet.** `PictureRenderer` takes a `Layout` with the parts' images. The page's renderer gives the template's data, and the composer gives a file. A public filter to swap the renderer waits for the 3D module that would use it, because a filter nobody calls is API to keep forever.
- **The layers' renditions are the plugin's own files.** They are made from the layer image at the picture's sizes, in AVIF and WebP, kept beside the composed pictures and named by a hash of the attachment, its last change and the size. Changing `image_editor_output_format` would change every upload in the shop.
- **A composed picture is asked for, never waited on.** These ask for one:
  - an add to the cart;
  - an Edit build that replaces;
  - a build link's page, where a crawler will read the share card.

  Each asks for its build once. Until the picture is made, the cart shows the deck's photo and the share card shows the deck's layer or photo.
- **The queue's cap and the folder's cap.** When the queue is full, nothing new is asked for, and the fallbacks above stand. That is how a stream of made-up build links stays cheap. The folder's cap is pruned oldest first. The numbers are set while building and recorded with what they cost.
- **GD and Imagick agree within a tolerance,** measured per channel on the same layout. The figure is set from the first comparison and recorded.
- **Share cards with another SEO plugin.** With Yoast, the tags go through its filters. With no SEO plugin, the plugin prints its own. With another known SEO plugin (Rank Math, SEOPress, All in One SEO), the plugin prints nothing rather than a second set of tags. Those plugins aren't tested here.
- **Baseplates (#39)** come from each maker's own pages, with the source and the day read, and stay empty wherever the maker gives no figure.

## Review

### What was built, against the plan

Every item under "What it builds" is built:

- **Owed first.** Shop Kit's column spans the row (D-184). The assembly switch is a button in a GET form. "What is in the box" is a visible heading. Issue #39's Independent baseplates are in (D-189); Thunder's and Venture's pages give none. Issues #40, #22 and #25 are closed. #36 stays open, quarantined, below.
- **The renderer interface** and the page's renderer draw the domain's `Layout` (D-195). There is no public filter.
- **In the page.** The board is layered `<picture>` elements with explicit sizes and AVIF and WebP sources. The deck's layer comes first, and each layer carries `cbuild_part_classes`. A pick swaps a layer in place, and the build is said once. A part with no layer image keeps its labelled photo, and the phone bar's small picture is the board.
- **The layers' renditions** are the plugin's own files, made in the part's index job (D-194). They go when the image is replaced or deleted (D-201).
- **The composer** draws with GD, or with Imagick where GD cannot (D-196). GD and Imagick agree within the tolerance, measured.
- **The jobs.** `cbuild_compose_picture` draws only for a build that passes validation, from its IDs, with the queue capped (D-197). `cbuild_prune_pictures` runs daily under the 200 MB cap. Site Health reports a host that cannot draw (D-198).
- **The admin:** the Layer image field (D-190), the kit's Picture section (D-192) and the Catalogue check's `layer_missing` (D-193).
- **The kit's cart thumbnail** shows in the Store API and the classic carts (D-199).
- **Share cards** go through Yoast's filters, or the plugin prints its own. Beside Rank Math, SEOPress or All in One SEO it prints none (D-200).
- **`docs/PICTURE.md`** is in, and **uninstall** removes the folder.

Where the result differs from the plan:

- **GD comes before Imagick** where both can draw, the other way round from the spec's order. GD is on every host WordPress supports, and the two agree within the tolerance (D-196).
- **The first unfurl.** A build link's page asks for its picture, and so does the state route's answer to a ready review, since that is where the link is offered (D-205, from the audit). Until the picture is made, the card shows the deck's product photo.
- **The queue is shared out.** A page's asks take at most 10 of the 50 places. Every job holds the folder to its cap, and a failed draw is logged (D-205, from the audit).
- **The largest paint is tested in the browser** as well as by Lighthouse. On a phone the deck's layer is the largest paint. On a desktop the step's heading is, and no image beats the layer (D-204).
- **The phone bar's contents sit at its foot.** The audit's full run found Next rising under a press in webkit-phone (D-206).
- **Test-first.** Each step's cases were committed with their code, with the red run in the commit's message (D-065). The audit's fixes each have a red run and a deliberate break, listed below.
- **The version is 0.7.0.** No run-time dependency and no table was added. The only dev tool added is `tools/imagick-coders.sh`, which adds ImageMagick's coders to wp-env's container (D-191).
